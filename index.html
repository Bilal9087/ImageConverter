How to Compress & Resize Multiple Images Online

  1. Upload Your Images: Drag and drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP files into the upload box above, or click Browse Files to select multiple images from your device.
  2. Adjust Quality & Scale: Use the Quality slider (80% is ideal for web speed) and Resize Scale slider to reduce pixel dimensions if needed.
  3. Choose Output Format: Select WebP for maximum compression, or keep JPEG / PNG based on your form or website requirements.
  4. Process & Download: Click Process Images to optimize the entire queue, then save files individually or click Download All (.zip).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is PixelPress completely free to use?

Yes, PixelPress is 100% free with no daily file limits, watermarks, or account registration required.

Are my personal photos or documents safe?

Completely safe. Because PixelPress processes images locally inside your web browser, your files never leave your computer or smartphone.

How can I reduce an image size under 50KB or 100KB for online forms?

Upload your photo, lower the Quality slider to around 50%–65%, and reduce the Resize Scale to 60%–75%. Click Process Images to check the exact new file size before downloading.

Why should I convert images to WebP format?

WebP images load significantly faster than traditional JPEG and PNG files, improving website page speed scores and search engine optimization (SEO).
